# Curriculum Overview — DAM & DAW First Year (Andalucía 2026/2027)

## Key Finding

**DAM and DAW share identical first-year modules.** The RAs, CEs, and contents are the same. The only difference is in pedagogical orientation references (which title objectives/competencies each module contributes to).

This means our platform can use **ONE set of curriculum content** tagged for both degrees.

## Platform Course Structure

```
Course (= Module)
  └── Block (= thematic unit, NOT official)
       └── Lesson (= learning sequence)
            └── ContentBlock (text, video, code, file, link)
            └── Exercise
            └── Practice
```

**Important:** The "blocks" and "units" are OUR pedagogical organization, not official curriculum sequences. The official curriculum defines RAs, CEs, and contents — not units.

## Pedagogical Approach

- Students are seeing these concepts for the FIRST TIME
- Explanations from zero, clear language
- Progressive examples, immediate practice
- Short exercises → challenges → review → project
- No infantilization — these are CFGS students

## Content Language

- **Code identifiers, comments:** English
- **Explanations, exercises, UI:** Spanish
- **Official references:** Spanish (original normativa)

## What's NOT in This Research (Yet)

- Second-year modules — different between DAM and DAW
- Concreción curricular del centro — specific to each educational center
- Módulo optativo content — varies by center
