## Notes

1. The BOE (RD 405/2023) defines the **minimum curriculum** — RAs, CEs, and contents
2. The **year distribution** (which modules in Year 1 vs Year 2) is defined by each autonomous community
3. The **Andalusia resolution** (July 2026) provides the specific distribution for 2026/2027
4. General modules (IPE1, Inglés, Digitalización, Sostenibilidad) are defined by autonomous communities, not in the BOE
5. The BOE's Orientaciones Pedagógicas differ between DAM and DAW for the same module — the RAs, CEs, and contents are identical
6. **Andalusia extends every module beyond the state minimum** — state hours are a floor, not the actual teaching load
7. Digitalización (1665) and Sostenibilidad (1708) are **second-year modules**, NOT first year
